# DOCTRINA — Epistemic and Source Boundaries

## Distinctions
- **Structural completeness**: the text or JSON identifies a claim, premises, warrant and links.
- **Internal inferential adequacy**: the reasoning is persuasive/valid *under its own stated assumptions and inference form*.
- **External support adequacy**: actual law, original philosophical texts, peer-reviewed scholarship or empirical evidence supports premises.
- **Normative persuasiveness**: a contested scholarly judgment, never inferred by graph connectivity.
- **Originality**: final, separately bounded comparative judgment, not the absence of matching titles.

## Source states
`inspected`: actual relevant passage and locator inspected; `partial`: segment inspected only; `metadata_only`: bibliographic existence confirmed; `unverified`: material unavailable or not examined. These states belong to each locator/passage, not only the document as a whole.

For an exact quotation, require literal passage, edition/translation, page or equivalent locator and an inspected source. For paraphrase, locate its propositional support, not just a bibliography entry. Never treat source authority as a substitute for sound inference.

## Review without the full manuscript
Review only the supplied scope. Identify dependencies that would require missing sections; classify as open, not contradictions or success. Preserve the author's exact text if provided; system-generated premise reconstruction must be tagged `proposed`.

## Internal first, originality last
Prior literature can inform conceptual interpretation from day one. The **global contribution/novelty adjudication** must wait until internal theses stabilize. If final novelty evaluation undermines the central thesis, return to argument construction and repeat final gate later; this is not circularity in the methodology but an iterative research process.

## Decisions and privacy
Only the researcher approves thesis revisions, major theoretical commitments and concessions to critical objections. Keep unfinished dissertations and manuscripts in private storage; public skill repository stores tools and anonymized examples only.
