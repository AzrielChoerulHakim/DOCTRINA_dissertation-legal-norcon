# DOCTRINA — Konstitusi metodologis (v0.5.0)

Approved project direction, 28 November 2026. DOCTRINA is the assistant's reusable skill and guide for constructing, developing and reviewing doctoral normative legal research, not a website, a generic essay generator, or a substitute for the researcher. Apply whenever asked to use DOCTRINA or construct a normative-law dissertation in this project. Load this constitution and the appropriate module before substantive work.

## Authority and scope
This constitution supersedes the two-object restriction in RFC-007 and any older instruction restricting DOCTRINA to reviewing existing manuscripts. Preserve P1 author-fidelity, source verification, confidentiality, institutional requirements and the final-originality gate. Exactly one primary object: norm, judgment, concept. The others may support it. Theories are instruments, not research objects. No theory-testing mandate or empirical novelty category.

- Norm: authoritative version, legal system, dates, hierarchy, conditions, exceptions, interpretation, normative conflict or justification.
- Judgment: court, case identifier/date, jurisdiction, procedural posture, actual legal questions, accepted facts, parties' submissions, majority/concurrence/dissent where relevant, reasons necessary to disposition versus other observations (where the system permits such a distinction), operative order and later status. Reconstruct ratio only from the actual judgment and explain uncertainties. No generalization from one decision without an additional warrant.
- Concept: actual legal uses, competing conceptions, assumptions, definitions, boundary cases and consequences. Distinguish stipulated definition from positive-law meaning.

## Research discipline
First identify object, exact locus, temporal/jurisdictional boundaries, problem and an explicit unknown. A bounded reconnaissance of primary materials and closest literature may precede the provisional thesis; it must answer the problem, not accumulate sources. Every subsequent search has a question or argument ID, purpose, expected evidence, stopping criterion, and records support, contrary evidence, or uncertainty. Off-scope leads go into a parking lot. Expand only with a recorded reason and reassess affected claims. Read opposing authorities and close scholarship, not merely corroborating sources. Primary texts before claims about their contents; no invented citations, unsupported legal status, or claims of exhaustive search.

## Contribution
Aim provisionally at normative (what ought to govern and why), interpretative (defensible distinctive reading), or conceptual (clarification, distinction or reconstruction). One candidate primary type, secondary optional; revise as warranted. The candidate is a research direction, not a novelty claim. Only after internal and dialectical review, integration, defense rehearsal and researcher sign-off may Module 07 compare actual propositions to the closest prior work. Missing decisive sources => indeterminate. No mandatory reform or general theory.

## Method
Use symbolic polysyllogistic construction or Derridean deconstruction as the primary analytic route; select based on object/question, not prestige. Combination is permitted only with distinct functions, ordered handoff, and an explicit bridge; neither mode automatically proves the conclusions of the other. Supporting legal interpretation methods may supply warrants. See modules/09-symbolic-polysyllogism.md and modules/10-derrida-deconstruction.md. Never force deductions or a contradiction onto the material. If the preferred route cannot responsibly answer the question, explain why and narrow or revise it.

## Construction
Use a versioned dependency ledger: I (core idea/problem), A (core argument/provisional thesis), SA (subargument), D (development), C (intermediate conclusion), S (final synthesis). Edges express support, derivation, constraint, test, revision or contribution; shared topic alone is not a connection. Distinguish organizational ancestry from actual inferential premises. Each subsection identifies proposition/question, materials and analytic operation, and consequence for its parent. Rejected premises invalidate dependent conclusions until reviewed. Preserve meaningful background only with a declared function. Keep dissent and negative findings.

## Workflow
Read modules/08-directed-construction.md. Work from zero or enter at the requested live stage; inspect prerequisites, mark missing evidence and do not pretend completion. Researcher controls thesis changes. In new construction distinguish assistant proposals from author assertions; P1 is mandatory when reconstructing a live manuscript, never fabricate author approval. No private manuscripts, full copyrighted PDFs or sensitive case data in public GitHub. Use local case files.

## Quality
Separate structural checks, semantic interpretation, inferential adequacy, source integrity, final originality and institutional judgment. Automated lint cannot establish legal correctness, textual fidelity, novelty or doctoral quality. Do not write beyond what has been analysed or conclude beyond the demonstrated reasons. Report blocked stages, residual objections and limits rather than hiding them in academic prose.
