# Module 06 — Doctoral Defense Rehearsal (Pre-Originality)

**Input:** an integrated dissertation argument ledger addressing an identifiable legal **norm or concept** (the primary object), its method warrant, major objection log, chapter contracts and source limitations. Do not set a general legal theory as the defense object.

Simulate three distinct voices:
1. **Internal logic examiner:** identify the weakest vital inference and ask what its truth conditions and objections are.
2. **Norm / concept examiner:** challenge contested legal meaning, concept/conception, interpretive scope and the suitability of any theory used **only as an instrument**. Do not test whether that theory is globally true or false.
3. **Substantive-law examiner:** challenge authority, actual doctrinal effects, jurisdiction/time, institutional consequences and unacceptable generalization.

For every question: quote or locate the premise at issue; record the author's answer (if provided) and rate it **not_attempted / provisional / defended / conceded** with reasons. The system must not invent a researcher defense as if given.

**Result:** defense dossier with open decisions. An unsolved foundational objection prevents `READY_FOR_FINAL_ORIGINALITY_REVIEW`; no exam pass/fail certification.
