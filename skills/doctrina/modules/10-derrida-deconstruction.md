# Module 10 — Pembacaan dekonstruktif Derrida

This is a situated reading protocol, not an algorithm that guarantees a contradiction. It must remain responsive to the text's singularity and context. Distinguish Derrida's wording, the legal object's wording and the researcher's reading.

1. Locate actual passages, edition/translation, page/paragraph and legal context; read surrounding text, repeat usages and competing passages. Establish a charitable ordinary reading before questioning it.
2. Identify a concrete distinction/opposition and any privilege or hierarchy if textually demonstrable. Examine what enables the favored term's meaning, what it excludes or treats as secondary, and where the excluded element is presupposed. Do not invent an opposition simply to fill the template.
3. Trace instability, supplement, trace, différance, iterability or aporia only where a specific inspected Derridean passage warrants their use. A tension is not necessarily a contradiction; an aporia is not simply a difficult decision or a formal logical inconsistency. Identify textual evidence and limits of each proposed reading.
4. Explore reversal and displacement where warranted; do not merely invert the hierarchy or assert that every reading is equally valid. Distinguish the textual finding from theoretical lens and subsequent legal consequence.
5. Test an alternative charitable reading, context or counter-passage that could weaken the proposed reading; preserve unresolved differences and negative findings.
6. Develop bounded interpretive/conceptual consequences; normative reconstruction requires an independently argued warrant. No obligation to produce legal reform, universal truth, or prescriptive closure.

Output: passage ID and locator | ordinary reading | proposed opposition and evidence | excluded/required element | tension and alternative reading | consequence and limit | dependent claim. If no tension is demonstrable, report that result. A combination with symbolic logic requires an explicit handoff: e.g. logical reconstruction of a judgment's reasons first, then textual examination of a distinction in those reasons; return any revised premises to logical review.

Reference notes in references/derrida-library.md are reading leads only; inspect actual editions before attribution.
