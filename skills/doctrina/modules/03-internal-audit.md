# Module 03 — Internal Argument Audit (Primary Engine)

**Priority:** the module receiving the greatest amount of attention and revision time for ongoing drafts.

## Sequence
A. **Charity:** restate the strongest *internally faithful* version of the author's argument and identify conjectured warrants separately.
B. **Inferential inspection:** test consequence (deductive/defeasible/interpretive/etc.) appropriate to claimed inference. Ask whether premises, if granted, justify the conclusion; then whether premises themselves are defended.
C. **Semantic stability:** inspect changes in terms, scope, time, subject of norm, sense of obligation, authority hierarchy and concept/conception distinction.
D. **Method consistency:** compare the argument against the project's own methodological warrant. Detect unsupported moves from empirical to conceptual or from descriptive to normative.
E. **Intra-textual coherence:** compare prior paragraphs, adjacent subarguments, earlier chapters and conclusion. Different quotations or provisional positions need not be contradictions.
F. **Immanent critique:** when a critical research mode is chosen, test target doctrine against its *own* stated commitments; a negative or aporetic result may be valid without a prescriptive solution.
G. **Repair options:** smallest edit to premise/warrant/qualifier; more radical theoretical reformulation; or explicit acknowledgment of limit.

**Required finding format:** code IA-01..IA-16, exact textual anchor, claim(s), demonstrated or hypothesized failure, severity, strongest possible reply, two repair routes, downstream nodes, reviewer uncertainty and decision owner.

**Never:** deem a theorem or legal theory true because its graph has no broken edges; force all philosophical arguments into syllogisms; treat all cyclical structures as fallacies.

**Gate:** clear foundational failures imply `INTERNAL_REVISION_REQUIRED`. Unknown source or absent sections imply constrained assessment, not exoneration.
