# Module 07 — Final Three-Type Originality / Contribution Audit

**Scope:** dissertation about a legal norm or legal concept ONLY. Theory may inform analysis, but is not a research object. For the approved design contract, read \`../../../docs/RFC-008-THREE-TYPE-NOVELTY-BASELINE.md\`.

## Activation strictly last

Do **not** assess or certify novelty before internal argument audit, dialectical testing, chapter integration, doctoral defense review and explicit researcher authorization. Reading literature, reconstructing prior positions and noticing similarities **continue throughout** earlier stages. The difference is a final evaluative verdict.

If any required gate is absent or there is an unaddressed foundational challenge, return \`NOT_READY\` and list precise blockers rather than offering a speculative novelty label as a result.

## Exactly three novelty categories

- **normative** — a defensible original normative construction, criterion, ordering, resolution or reformulation for the examined norm or concept, with a justified legal/normative basis and legal consequences. Neither a new statutory draft nor a general theory is mandatory.
- **interpretative** — a distinct and well-defended interpretation of an identified legal norm/concept against the strongest earlier readings, with a legally material difference. Mere disagreement is not novelty.
- **conceptual** — a precise clarification, delimitation, differentiation or reconstruction of an identified legal concept that addresses a demonstrated problem and has a substantive jurisprudential implication. Mere renaming is not novelty.

Select **exactly one primary novelty category**. An optional secondary category must be supported by a separate and traceable contribution. A norm-object can yield conceptual novelty; a concept-object can yield normative novelty. Do not confuse object and contribution categories.

## Method

1. Confirm the stabilized author-approved central thesis and its necessary proposition IDs, with precise draft locators and object classification \`norm | concept\`.
2. Verify that reviewed literature covers major relevant positions, directly inspected sources, earlier and coauthored work by the author, and relevant valid versions of normative sources. Disclose coverage and access limitations.
3. Compare **proposition with proposition**, not keyword, title, wording or topic. Mark for each contribution candidate: already present / materially equivalent / narrower / broader / differently justified / newly applied / presently unverified. Don't equate any of these statuses with a finding of novelty until justified.
4. Choose one **primary** category by the actual **kind of legal contribution** (normative, interpretative, conceptual); optionally choose one secondary if independently defended.
5. Explain the strongest prior position, the precise delta, the justification of that delta and its legally material implications. Identify counterarguments and any reasonable alternative classification.
6. Return one status: \`CANDIDATE\`, \`SUPPORTED_WITH_LIMITATIONS\`, \`INSUFFICIENTLY_SUPPORTED\`, or \`INDETERMINATE\`. Use \`NOT_READY\` if the audit could not legitimately start.
7. If final literature comparison defeats a supposed contribution, reopen argument reconstruction/integration and re-check its dependencies; do **not** invent a replacement contribution.

## Required output

- Research object and thesis IDs.
- Final gate readiness and researcher authorization.
- Exactly one primary novelty type, only **if** a defensible candidate exists; secondary optional.
- Prior-proposition comparison matrix with exact verified locators and noted unavailable sources.
- Argumentative justification, substantive legal effect, strongest objection and scope of claims.
- Final bounded status, unresolved questions and evidence limitations.

Never claim an automated novelty percentage, a certified globally new theory, a university pass, or originality by search absence. A well-written but previously defended proposition may be **insufficiently supported**, not automatically novel.
