# Module 07 — FINAL Originality / Contribution Audit

**Activation strictly last:** only after internal audit, dialectical review, chapter integration and defense rehearsal establish a provisional stable thesis. It is **not** the first activity; reading and debating prior work began much earlier.

Before starting, verify the researcher's recorded authorization and the `READY_FOR_FINAL_ORIGINALITY_REVIEW` gate. If not ready, return `ORIGINALITY_REVIEW_INCOMPLETE` and name exact blockers.

1. State the *stabilized* central thesis about an identified legal **norm or concept** and its necessary propositions. Any broader claim to originate a legal theory is outside the current DOCTRINA scope.
2. Extract relevant propositions from directly checked prior studies, including the researcher's earlier/collaborative publications.
3. Compare contributions concerning that **norm or concept** at propositional level: already-present, clarified, extended, critiqued, newly justified, or presently unverified.
4. Identify provenance, dates, edition, coauthorship and access limits. Mark source inspection gaps.
5. Evaluate novelty candidate as limited epistemic claim; never infer uniqueness from missing search results.
6. If novelty comparison undermines the argument or its contribution, route back to Modules 02–05 and repeat internal/defense cycle after revision.

**Output:** bounded contribution matrix + confidence limitations, **not** a global certificate of doctoral originality.
