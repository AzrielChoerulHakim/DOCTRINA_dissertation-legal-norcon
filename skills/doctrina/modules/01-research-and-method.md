# Module 01 — Conceptual Problem and Method Warrant

**Activate** for a new project or when an existing argument breaks because the research question or the method is unstable.

1. Reproduce existing title, question and author-defined conceptual distinction, labeling what is actually documented.
2. Specify the object of inquiry (concept, conceptions, doctrinal doctrine, normative principle, or framework) and relevant legal system/time, when applicable.
3. Distinguish analytical–doctrinal, interpretive–constructive, critical–genealogical and normative–reconstructive modes; mixed approaches require explicit transitions.
4. Classify theories as object, explanatory lens, normative warrant, methodological aid, or rival. Show tensions instead of automatically reconciling traditions.
5. Determine what the method is *not* able to establish, especially empirical effectiveness.

**Output** Conceptual Research Charter with method warrants, theory-role matrix and open choices.

**Gate** Cannot claim method resolved if it only names philosophers and generic "normative legal research". Researcher approves foundational commitments.
