# Module 01 — Research object: NORMA / PUTUSAN / KONSEP

Read ../CONSTITUTION.md and Module 08. Declare exactly one primary legal object; the others may be supporting materials. No whole-theory testing or mandatory theory invention. State a bounded question, actual source locus, jurisdiction and temporal scope, unknowns, initial idea and proposed argument.

## Norm
Locate authoritative text/version, hierarchy, applicability, conditions/exceptions and affected positions. Identify ambiguity, conflict, gap, interpretive disagreement or deficient justification. Test warrant and rival reading; distinguish legal force from legitimacy and normative preference.

## Judgment
Locate court, case number, date, procedural posture, authoritative full judgment and later status. Separate submissions, accepted facts, issues, reasoning, operative order and separate opinions where present. Identify reasoning necessary to disposition where the system permits; do not force common-law ratio categories onto another jurisdiction. Test relation between grounds and order, alternatives, precedential weight and limits of generalization. Do not infer accepted facts from parties' claims or assume finality.

## Concept
Identify actual legal usages, competing conceptions, definitions, conditions and hard cases. Examine equivocation, assumptions, boundaries and normative implications. Distinguish stipulation from interpretation of existing law.

## Method and gate
Choose symbolic polysyllogism (Module 09), deconstruction (Module 10), or an explicitly staged combination. Supporting doctrinal methods supply legal warrants. Explain fit, route, limitations, sources and effects on dependent chapters. A philosopher's name is not a legal research object. Record the charter before widening research. A critical outcome may remain nonprescriptive.
