# Module 05 — Live Dissertation Integration & Revision Impact

**Primary context:** a dissertation continuously being written, often with already approved or partly drafted chapters.

## Unit-to-whole trace
`Sentence → Argument Unit → Subsection → Chapter Thesis → Central Thesis`. Every chapter must have an argumentative task; a background section can be necessary without independently proving the thesis.

**For each chapter contract:** central question, chapter proposition, primary arguments, necessary preceding conclusions, strongest unresolved objection, effect on central thesis, source limitations, and next chapter dependency.

**Versioned loop:** on changing any claim, warrant, theory-role or definition, record *before/after* and researcher authorization. Traverse downstream dependencies and mark affected chapter contracts `RE_REVIEW_REQUIRED`. A changed thesis cannot silently inherit old approvals. If a new chapter contradicts an earlier chapter, preserve both positions as `unresolved` until author elects revised thesis or deliberate dialectical staging.

**Progress snapshots:** `in_progress`, `internally_stable_with_limitations`, `dialectically_reviewed`, `integrated`, `defense_ready`, `novelty_reviewed` — these describe workflow state, not academic grades.

## Originality sequencing
No final originality declaration during the authoring loop. Prior literature still supports genealogy, source critique and fair rival reconstruction. Go to Module 07 only when chapter spine is integrated and defense questions are adequately answered.
