# Module 04 — Dialectical Stress and Boundary Cases

Do this **after** internal reconstruction so external criticism does not rewrite the researcher's thesis before it is understood.

1. **Steelman** the strongest opposing interpretation or normative account from inspected relevant texts.
2. Separate attacks on premises, inference rule, definition, scope, background assumption, and consequence.
3. Construct limiting/boundary cases that bear directly on the target conception; label hypothetical cases as hypothetical.
4. Distinguish rebutting argument (supports opposite conclusion) from undercutting argument (weakens warrant).
5. Propose a reasoned reply or concession and ask whether the argument survives through qualification.
6. Re-run internal audit if a new qualifier alters the claim's scope.

**Output:** ranked objection ledger, response proposals, unresolved major/foundational challenges, decision log.

**Gate:** serious open objections prevent readiness for final originality review unless explicitly narrowed and justified by researcher and reviewer. This gate does not demand universal consensus.
